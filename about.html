About Mood To Music

Music that matches how you feel. Mood To Music is a music experience designed to connect emotions with songs and help users discover music based on their current mood.

🎵 What is Mood To Music?

Mood To Music is a web-based music application that recommends music according to the user's emotions and preferences. Instead of searching for a song only by its title or artist, users can start with how they feel and discover music that fits their mood.


The project provides different ways to discover music, including mood selection, an interactive mood quiz, artist-based browsing and music categories. Users can also play songs, manage favourites, view listening history and download available music.

👤 Login & Guest Experience

Mood To Music supports both registered users and guest users. Logged-in users can keep information such as favourites and listening history using the application's storage system.


Guests can explore and enjoy the application during their current session. Guest session data is temporary and is not intended to remain after the session ends.

💫 Our Goal

Our goal is to make music discovery more personal, simple and enjoyable by connecting music with emotions. Whether someone feels happy, sad, lonely, excited, in love or broken, Mood To Music helps them find something to listen to.
